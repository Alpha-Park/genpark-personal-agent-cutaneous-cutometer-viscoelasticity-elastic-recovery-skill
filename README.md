# Cutaneous Cutometer Skin Viscoelasticity & Suction Mechanical Deformation Skill

A production-grade, zero-mock personal agent skill engineered for dermatological suction elastometry, cutaneous biomechanics, and dermal extracellular matrix elasticity profiling.

## Features
- **Comprehensive Deformation Analysis**: Computes immediate ($U_e$), delayed ($U_v$), and total ($U_f$) suction displacements.
- **Biomechanical Ratio Calculation**: Computes $R_2$ (gross elasticity), $R_5$ (net elasticity), $R_6$ (viscoelastic ratio), and $R_7$ (biological elasticity).
- **Pure Standard Library**: Zero external dependencies; fully portable for edge and clinical AI deployment.

## Clinical References
- Dobrev, H. (2002). *Use of Cutometer to assess skin mechanical properties*. In *Bioengineering of the Skin: Skin Biomechanics* (pp. 235-256). CRC Press.
- Krueger, N., et al. (2011). *Age-related changes in skin mechanical properties: a study using the Cutometer*. Skin Research and Technology, 17(2), 141-148.
